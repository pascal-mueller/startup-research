## Research limitations / uncertainties
- WebFetch and curl were blocked by the egress policy for every domain tried (bis.org, financialprofessionals.org, pwc, eact.eu, six-group, snb). All new sources were verified from web-search results returned from the publisher's own page (or a press-release wire for Hackett). The web-search budget for the session was then exhausted, so a few points are stated as established concepts without a numeric citation (e.g. CLS settlement, T+2 conventions, EBICS/SWIFT basics, deposit protection via the companies-size glossary term).
- MillTech (hedge ratio ~48%, tenor ~5.3 months) is a vendor survey of mid-sized corporates — labelled as indicative.
- Swiss withholding tax / 10/20 non-bank rules: the Bär & Karrer briefing is from 2019 and the rules have been under reform; the page only says they "shape" pool design and that tax review is required — no rule detail is asserted. Verify the current status if someone expands cash pooling content.
- Fictional-company numbers introduced on these pages (consistent with companies.yaml, but new): Helvetic DSO ~70 / DIO ~130 / DPO ~40 days; Alpine CCC ~120 days; GlobalChem CCC ~70 days; Helvetic RCF margin 0.90% and commitment fee 35% of margin (from existing glossary); Helvetic hedge policy bands 60–90% next quarter / 30–60% quarters 2–4; Helvetic counterparty limits CHF 30m (A) / CHF 15m (BBB) (from existing glossary); Kleio USD 4m MMF, CHF 8m laddered deposits; HM Suzhou CNY 44m ≈ CHF 5m; Helvetic RCF bank wallet-share table; GlobalChem 60–80% fixed debt target; GlobalChem EUR 200–500m CP outstanding. Other streams should reuse rather than contradict these.
- Option premium for Alpine's bid ("1–2% of notional") is explicitly illustrative.
