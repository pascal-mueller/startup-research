## Critical errors (must fix)

**1. Helvetic financials contradict the canonical facts (three numbers wrong in one paragraph).**
`content/pages/companies/helvetic.mdx` (the position section): *"With EBITDA of about CHF 85m and net debt of about CHF 119m (RCF 60 + bond 100 − cash 41), net debt / EBITDA is roughly 1.4x against the 3.25x covenant"*. Canonical (AUTHORING.md, "Canonical facts clarified after exemplar review", 30 Sep 2026 LTM): **EBITDA ≈ CHF 78m, net debt ≈ CHF 106m, net debt/EBITDA ≈ 1.36x**. The page is wrong on all three, and the size/index and mid-market pages inherit the "comfortable at 1.4x" story. The arithmetic on the page is internally consistent (60 + 100 − 41 = 119) but the inputs clash with the canonical set; to reach ≈106 net debt the group position (cash ≈ 41) or the drawn amounts must change — the illustrative "Group cash 41" table is itself in tension with the canonical net debt. Fix direction: rebuild the example to the canonical trio (78 / 106 / 1.36x); e.g. adjust the group cash line (canonical implies cash ≈ CHF 54m, or state the drawn RCF as CHF 35m + EUR 27m ≈ CHF 62m and pick the cash figure that lands on 106), and re-derive the ratio. Every page and compare cell that quotes these numbers must move together (also check `content/data/orgs.yaml`, `content/workflows/covenant-monitoring.yaml` — cross-stream request).

**2. RCF maturity is stated as 2027; canonical is Oct 2028.**

## Missing concepts

- **Helvetic reports under Swiss GAAP FER, GlobalChem under IFRS** (AUTHORING canonical). Never mentioned in the module. It matters twice: hedge accounting ("designated as cash-flow hedges", `helvetic.mdx`; `co-fx-hedging`) is a far less automatic choice under FER than under IFRS 9, and covenant EBITDA/net debt definitions ("frozen GAAP", what counts as cash) are a real interview topic. One sentence each would ground both pages.
- **The RCF drawn split and rollover mechanics (CHF 35m + EUR 27m, interest periods rolling on the 15th)** — canonical detail absent from `helvetic.mdx` and `co-investments` ("reducing RCF drawings at the next rollover"). This is exactly the concrete that makes "surplus CHF repays drawings" teachable (why the 15th matters; the EUR 27m drawing means Helvetic is structurally short EUR, matching the "German payroll funded from the parent's EUR account" story).
- **Graded hedge bands by tenor.** Canonical: Q+1 60–90%, Q+2 40–70%, Q+3 25–55%, Q+4 0–40%. The module teaches a single "60–90% of next-quarter forecast net inflows" band (`helvetic.mdx` workflow table and typical problems; `co-fx-hedging`). The graduated band is the actual teaching point (how much uncertainty to hedge at each tenor) and is what differentiates Helvetic from GlobalChem's "layered 12–18 month programmes". See also major issue 6 below.
- **What the analyst's covenant model actually contains** (add-backs, definition of net debt, which cash is deducted) — `helvetic.mdx` says "Lea calculates net debt / EBITDA semi-annually from Claudia's figures" but the canonical ratios (78 / 106 / 1.36x) give a free worked example that is currently missing from the covenant row.

## Unrealistic workflow descriptions

- **Lea's payment authority contradicts itself in four places** (this also breaks the module's own segregation-of-duties teaching):
  - `helvetic.mdx`, roles table: Lea *"prepares treasury payments (intercompany loans, deal settlements) for Daniel's and Thomas's release"* and Daniel *"approves as second signatory for the parent's payments"*.
  - `helvetic.mdx`, workflow table: *"Large payment approval … Lea checks and releases."*
  - `co-payment-approval` helvetic cell: *"Lea releases it through the connectivity tool before the CHF cut-off"* (fine as execution after CFO/CEO approval — but the same paragraph needs the two-signature logic to survive).
  - `co-payment-run` helvetic cell: *"the accounting team prepares batches and Daniel or Lea approves as second signatory in the connectivity tool"* — a preparer approving as second signature is the classic control failure a practitioner would flag instantly.
  Fix direction: pick one model consistent with the canonical ("Lea prepares, Daniel decides"): e.g. accounting prepares → Lea checks/treasuries release (Daniel or Thomas co-sign per matrix) and never "Daniel or Lea".
- **"Weekly: German payroll (about EUR 4.2m)"** (`helvetic.mdx`, cash-flow pattern). German payroll is monthly (Gehaltszahlung, typically end of month); putting it under a "Weekly" heading implies weekly wage runs. Either move it to the monthly bullet ("HM Deutschland is short in payroll week, funded from the parent's EUR account") or explain that some German blue-collar staff are paid on a different cycle — as written a German interviewee would object.
- `co-fx-hedging` says Daniel *"executes rolling forwards monthly"* while `helvetic.mdx` says the hedge roll is a monthly event for "the quarter coming into the 60–90% band" — consistent with each other, but neither matches a four-quarter graded-band policy (see missing concepts). As written Helvetic's "policy" only ever covers one quarter ahead, which understates the mid-market model the size page claims ("written policy with hedge-ratio bands").
