## What this section covers

Treasury is a data-assembly job before it is a decision job. Every morning someone has to collect balances from every bank, payments due from the ERP, deals from their own records and forecasts from the business, check that nothing is missing, and only then decide what to move, fund, invest or hedge. The systems described here are the machinery for that assembly — and the places where it breaks.

## Five things that surprise newcomers

1. **[Files, not APIs](why:systems-files-not-apis), still carry most corporate banking data.** Statements arrive as end-of-day files ([camt.053](term:camt-053), [MT940](term:mt940), [BAI2](term:bai2)); payments leave as files ([pain.001](term:pain-001)). [Bank APIs](term:api-banking) are growing, especially for intraday balances, but at most companies they complement rather than replace file channels — below mid-market the API route is a platform one, such as SIX bLink inside the accounting software.
2. **The ERP is the source of most forecast data but knows nothing about the bank in real time.** It learns about cash when statements are imported and posted, usually the next morning.
3. **"We have a TMS" does not mean "we don't use Excel".** Even large treasuries keep scenario models, board packs and exception lists in spreadsheets. Below mid-market, the spreadsheet *is* the treasury system.
4. **Connectivity is a per-bank project.** Each bank requires contracts, onboarding, key exchange or certificates, format testing and user administration. Adding a bank or an acquired subsidiary means repeating it.
5. **Most automation failures are data failures.** Missing statements, unmapped [bank transaction codes](term:bank-transaction-code), truncated [remittance information](term:remittance-information), late or copied forecast submissions — see [Data quality](/systems/data-quality).

## Standards timeline you will hear about (2025–2026)

| Change | Status as of autumn 2026 | Why a treasurer cares |
|---|---|---|
| Swift MT → [ISO 20022](term:iso-20022) for cross-border interbank payments and reporting (CBPR+) | Coexistence ended 22 November 2025: payment MTs replaced by ISO 20022, reporting/statement MTs **deprecated** — still processed, no longer maintained[](cite:swift-iso-mt9xx,jpm-iso-2026) | Richer data travels through the chain; corporates are pushed to send structured data in pain.001 |
| MT940/MT942 statements | In CBPR+ scope but deprecated, not withdrawn, at the Nov 2025 milestone; the interbank reporting coexistence end is currently communicated as **November 2028** (per bank communications); corporate-to-bank delivery follows each bank's own timeline — Swiss banks quote no fixed end date[](cite:jpm-iso-faq-2026,swift-iso-mt9xx,zkb-iso20022-factsheet) | Many companies still receive MT940 from some banks and camt.053 from others — both must be mapped. [Why MT940 outlives the cut-over](why:systems-mt940-coexistence) |
| Swiss ISO 20022 versions | 2009 message versions discontinued in Switzerland — SIX dates the switch at **14 November 2026**, some banks communicate 21 November 2026[](cite:six-sps-cash-mgmt,zkb-iso20022-factsheet) [why two dates](why:systems-swiss-2026-date) | ERP/TMS formats must be on 2019 versions (pain.001.001.09, camt.05x.001.08) — [why the sunset date bites](why:systems-swiss-2009-sunset) |
| EBICS 3.0 in Switzerland | At least one major Swiss bank supports the 2019 ISO versions only via EBICS 3.0 from November 2026 and discontinued EBICS 2.4 in November 2025[](cite:zkb-ebics3) | EBICS client software must be upgraded; keys re-initialised in some cases |
| [Instant payments](term:real-time-payments) — EU Instant Payments Regulation, Swiss SIC Instant Payments | EU: euro-area banks must receive SCT Inst since Jan 2025 and send since Oct 2025, with [Verification of Payee](term:verification-of-payee) from Oct 2025; Swiss: SIC Instant Payments live since Aug 2024, reception mandatory for all remaining Swiss banks by end-2026[](cite:ecb-ipr,six-instant-payments) | 24/7, irrevocable settlement: [no overnight window](why:systems-instant-payments-24-7) for fraud checks or positioning; bank cut-offs matter less on instant rails |
| [Swift gpi](term:swift-gpi) payment tracking | Every cross-border payment carries a UETR; status updates, deducted charges and applied FX flow back via the bank's portal or API[](cite:ubs-gpi-corporates) | "Where is my payment?" becomes a lookup of the tracker instead of an email chain with the bank |
| Structured addresses | Unstructured postal addresses being phased out in payment messages; Swift deferred parts of the CBPR+ requirement (revised date due by Dec 2026) and unstructured may continue until at least November 2027; Swiss/SEPA Nov 2026 dates unchanged[](cite:jpm-iso-2026,postfinance-iso-updates) | Vendor master data in ERPs needs clean, structured addresses |
| SAP ECC end of mainstream maintenance | End of 2027 (optional extended maintenance to 2030)[](cite:sap-ecc-maintenance) | S/4HANA migrations reopen "which TMS, which connectivity" questions |
