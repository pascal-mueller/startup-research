## What the Group Treasurer's day is made of

Anna's department has five teams — cash & liquidity / [in-house bank](term:in-house-bank) (Priya), financial risk (Jonas), corporate finance (Olivier), treasury operations and the payment factory in Kraków (Marta), and treasury systems (Ben) — plus a [regional treasury centre](term:regional-treasury-centre) in Singapore. The daily position, the pools, the payments and the hedges are run by her teams. Her own work sits in four places:

- **Funding and the balance sheet:** bond refinancing, the [RCF](term:rcf), [commercial paper](term:commercial-paper) strategy, the [credit rating](term:credit-rating).
- **Banks:** the panel of relationship banks, who gets which business in return for their credit commitment ([wallet share](term:wallet-share)).
- **Policy and control:** limits, exceptions, fraud incidents, audit, systems risk.
- **Communication upward:** the CFO, the board and audit committee, investor relations, rating agencies.

<Callout type="confusion" title="The most senior person is not the best source on the daily process">

Anna reads Priya's liquidity dashboard every day, but she has not built a cash position herself in years. If you want to know how the morning actually works at GlobalChem, interview [Priya](/day/priya) or one of her cash managers. Ask Anna about funding, banks, policy, risk appetite and what she escalates to the CFO.

</Callout>

## Interviewing someone like Anna

<Callout type="interview" title="Good questions — and what she won't know">

- "What were the last three decisions that came to you rather than being taken by your team heads?"
- "How do you decide which bank gets the next mandate?"
- "What does your monthly report to the CFO contain, and what does he actually read?"
- "What was the last control failure or near miss, and what did you change afterwards?"
- "Which parts of your department's work would you never let a system do without a person?"

Anna is the best source on **funding, rating, bank relationships, policy, organisation design and budget for treasury technology**. She is a poor source on how long the morning position takes, which statements fail, or how subsidiaries fill in forecast templates. See [Head of Treasury role](/org/roles-head-of-treasury).

</Callout>
