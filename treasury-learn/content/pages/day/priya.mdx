## How Priya's day differs from Lea's

Priya does the same *job* as [Lea](/day/lea) and [Daniel](/day/daniel) together — know where the cash is, decide what to do with it — at thirty times the scale. The difference is not that the work is harder but that it is **organised differently**:

| | Helvetic (Lea + Daniel) | GlobalChem (Priya's team) |
|---|---|---|
| Statement collection | Connectivity tool + Excel; missing files chased by hand | TMS imports ~280 statements; missing files show up on an exception list |
| Moving cash | Manual intercompany loans, one decision at a time | Automatic [sweeps](term:sweeping) into EUR/USD pools; manual funding only for accounts outside the pools |
| Surplus | Parent's CHF used to reduce RCF drawings; little to invest | EUR/USD surplus placed in [MMFs](term:money-market-fund) within limits; CP repaid or rolled |
| Subsidiary funding | Intercompany loan agreements, booked by accounting | [In-house bank](term:in-house-bank) accounts and loans with monthly statements and interest |
| Decisions escalated | To the CFO | To the Group Treasurer, above Priya's limits |

## Interviewing someone like Priya

<Callout type="interview" title="Good questions — and what she won't know">

- "What was on this morning's exception list, and which ones reached you personally?"
- "How do you decide how much commercial paper to roll versus repay?"
- "When a subsidiary asks for money, what do you need to see, and who else has to agree?"
- "Which accounts are still outside the pools, and why?"
- "What part of the position do you still check by hand even though the TMS shows it?"

A cash & liquidity head is the best source on **daily liquidity, pooling, the in-house bank, short-term investments and CP**, and a good one on forecasting quality by entity. For FX hedging ask [Jonas's team](/org/roles-fx-risk); for payment execution ask [Marta](/org/roles-operations); for funding strategy ask [Anna](/day/anna).
