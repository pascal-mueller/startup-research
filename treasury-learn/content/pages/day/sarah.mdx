## How treasury fits into Sarah's job

Sarah's calendar is dominated by the board, fundraising, pricing, hiring and the annual audit. Treasury shows up in four forms: a weekly look at the bank balances and the [runway](term:cash-runway); approving payments that Nina, the accountant, prepares; a handful of decisions per quarter (where to hold the cash, whether to take [venture debt](term:venture-debt), when to convert USD); and reacting when something unusual happens. She has no [TMS](term:tms), no daily [cash position](term:cash-position), no hedging policy and no treasury analyst. Everything below would look very different at [Alpine](/day/petra) or [Helvetic](/day/daniel).

<Callout type="confusion" title="Runway is not a 13-week forecast">

A startup CFO forecasts **months of runway** from a monthly model (cash ÷ net burn, adjusted for known big receipts). A mid-market treasury builds a **13-week forecast by entity and currency**. Asking Sarah about "forecast accuracy by week" will get a puzzled look; asking "what would make you start fundraising three months earlier?" will not. See [cash forecasting — by size](wf:cash-forecasting).

</Callout>

## Interviewing someone like Sarah

<Callout type="interview" title="Good questions — and what she won't know">

- "Walk me through the last time you checked the bank balances. Which portals, in what order, what did you write down?"
- "How did you decide how to split the Series B money between banks? Who asked you to?"
- "Who can release a payment, and what happens when a new supplier's bank details arrive?"
- "What did you do with the venture-debt offer, and what did the board ask?"
- "What treasury task would you hand to someone else first if you could?"

Sarah is the right interviewee for **startup cash, runway, bank selection and fraud controls in a tiny team**. She is the wrong person for questions about TMS selection, hedge accounting, cash pooling or payment factories — she has never needed them. See [CFO role](/org/roles-cfo) and [who knows what](/org/who-knows-what).

</Callout>
