## Who owns this category inside the customer

[Working capital](term:working-capital) programmes touch three functions. AR/credit control owns collections; AP and procurement own supplier terms and early-payment programmes; treasury funds programmes, watches the effect on liquidity and [DSO](term:dso)/[DPO](term:dpo), and sometimes negotiates the bank side of [supply-chain finance](term:supply-chain-finance). See [treasury and AP](/org/with-ap) and [treasury and AR](/org/with-ar).

| Product type | Daily user | Treasury's role | Workflows |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR / collections automation (e.g. HighRadius, Tesorio) | AR team | Consumes better receipt forecasts | [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting) |
| [Dynamic discounting](term:dynamic-discounting) / early-payment marketplace (e.g. C2FO, Taulia) | AP, procurement | Decides whether surplus cash earns more in discounts than in deposits | [Surplus cash investment](wf:surplus-cash-investment), [liquidity planning](wf:liquidity-planning) |
| Bank-funded supply-chain finance (e.g. Taulia, bank programmes) | Procurement, AP | Negotiates bank lines; watches disclosure and rating-agency treatment | [Liquidity planning](wf:liquidity-planning), [credit facilities](wf:credit-facility-management) |

<Callout type="confusion" title="Don't pitch a working-capital product to the treasurer alone">

A treasurer may care about the outcome (cash) but not own the process or budget. If your interviews are about working capital, include AR, AP and procurement leads — and ask who signed the last contract of this kind.

</Callout>
