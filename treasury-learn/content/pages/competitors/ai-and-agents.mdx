## Say it plainly

<Callout type="warning" title="What was and was not verified">

- **Verified (from search snippets):** GTreasury's January 2026 "Ripple Treasury" launch was reported as including "AI-driven cash forecasting" alongside stablecoin settlement — a vendor claim as reported[](cite:comp-ripple-treasury-launch).
- **Not verified:** the scope of any other vendor's AI features (Kyriba, HighRadius, Trovata and others market AI capabilities — background knowledge), and **whether any AI-native startup ships a product that performs treasury tasks end-to-end**. Palm, Nilus, Statement and Millimetric were named in the research brief; nothing about them could be confirmed and they appear only as watchlist entries.

So this page does not tell you that treasury agents exist, and it does not tell you that they don't. It tells you how to find out.

</Callout>

## Features vs agents — the distinction that matters

| | AI feature / copilot | [Treasury agent](term:treasury-agent) |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Predicts (a forecast line), classifies (a bank transaction), answers a question about data | Performs a multi-step task: gathers data, applies rules or judgement, drafts or takes an action |
| Where it lives | Inside a TMS, forecasting or AR product | Standalone product or a mode inside one |
| Human role | Reads the output and decides | Reviews, approves, or handles exceptions |
| Evidence of reality | A demo of the feature on the customer's own data | A customer who can show what the agent did last week and what they corrected |
| Typical workflows | [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting), [bank reconciliation](wf:bank-reconciliation) matching | [Daily cash positioning](wf:daily-cash-positioning) preparation, variance explanation for [month-end reporting](wf:month-end-reporting), [bank fee analysis](wf:bank-fee-analysis) |

<Callout type="synthesis" title="Why claims in this area need extra scrutiny">

Treasury has two properties that make AI claims hard to judge from the outside. First, correctness is often only visible later (a forecast is judged weeks afterwards; a hedge decision months afterwards). Second, the consequential actions — releasing payments, dealing FX, moving cash between entities — sit behind approval chains and bank mandates that a vendor cannot remove. A product can therefore be "agentic" in the demo and a copilot in production. EACT's 2025 survey of European treasurers ranked AI low as a priority, citing missing data foundations and non-standardised data[](cite:eact-2025) — a reminder that the data problem precedes the model problem.

</Callout>

## How to verify an "agent" claim

<Callout type="interview" title="Questions for a vendor demo">

- "Show me this running on a customer's live bank data, not a demo tenant."
- "Which steps happen without a human clicking? Which system credentials does it hold? Can it release a payment?"
- "What happens when a bank statement is missing at 08:00?"
- "How do you measure whether it was right? Show me the error log from last month."

</Callout>

<Callout type="interview" title="Questions for a treasurer who uses one">

- "What did the tool do this morning before you logged in? What did you change?"
- "Has it ever been wrong in a way that mattered? How did you notice?"
- "What would you never let it do?"
- "If it disappeared tomorrow, what would you have to do by hand?"

</Callout>
