## Say it plainly

<Callout type="warning" title="What is claimed, what is sourced, what is not known">

- **Sourced — vendor claims as reported:** GTreasury's January 2026 "Ripple Treasury" launch was reported as including "AI-driven cash forecasting" alongside stablecoin settlement[](cite:comp-ripple-treasury-launch).
- **Sourced — the claims themselves:** **Nilus** self-describes as "your agentic workforce for treasury. AI agents reconcile cash, forecast liquidity, and automate finance ops across every bank and ERP"[](cite:comp-nilus-agents), and raised USD 10m led by Felicis in January 2025 (total ~USD 18.6m)[](cite:comp-nilus-funding-2025). **Palm** self-describes as "AI cash flow forecasting for treasury teams" that "sits on top of your TMS/ERP"[](cite:comp-palm-product) and raised a USD 6.1m seed in September 2024[](cite:comp-palm-seed-2024). These are live vendor self-descriptions — [the right evidence for what is claimed, not for what works](why:comp-self-description-bar).
- **Classified from those claims:** Palm's own words make it a forecasting layer over an existing system — an **AI feature**, not an agent ([why](why:comp-palm-feature-not-agent)). Nilus claims task performance and is the one entry in the agent column.
- **Not established:** whether any of this runs in production without a human re-doing the work. **Statement, Millimetric, Bound and Treasury4/TreasuryGo** remain watchlist names — nothing about them could be confirmed.

So this page does not tell you that treasury agents work, and it does not tell you that they can't. It tells you exactly who claims what, at what evidence level, and how to find out the rest.

</Callout>

## Features vs agents — the distinction that matters

| | AI feature / copilot | [Treasury agent](term:treasury-agent) |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Predicts (a forecast line), classifies (a bank transaction), answers a question about data | Performs a multi-step task: gathers data, applies rules or judgement, drafts or takes an action |
| Where it lives | Inside a TMS, forecasting or AR product | Standalone product or a mode inside one |
| Human role | Reads the output and decides | Reviews, approves, or handles exceptions |
| Evidence of reality | A demo of the feature on the customer's own data | A customer who can show what the agent did last week and what they corrected |
| Typical workflows | [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting), [bank reconciliation](wf:bank-reconciliation) matching | [Daily cash positioning](wf:daily-cash-positioning) preparation, variance explanation for [month-end reporting](wf:month-end-reporting), [bank fee analysis](wf:bank-fee-analysis) |

<Callout type="synthesis" title="Why claims in this area need extra scrutiny">

Treasury has two properties that make AI claims hard to judge from the outside. First, correctness is often only visible later (a forecast is judged weeks afterwards; a hedge decision months afterwards). Second, the consequential actions — releasing payments, dealing FX, moving cash between entities — sit behind approval chains and bank mandates that a vendor cannot remove. A product can therefore be "agentic" in the demo and a copilot in production. EACT's 2025 survey ranks cash-flow forecasting first and treasury technology review/replacement third among European treasurers' priorities[](cite:eact-2025); whether AI ranks low *because* data is fragmented was not confirmed against the primary text — but the data-first sequencing is the right question to put to interviewees.

</Callout>

## How to verify an "agent" claim

<Callout type="interview" title="Questions for a vendor demo">

- "Show me this running on a customer's live bank data, not a demo tenant."
- "Which steps happen without a human clicking? Which system credentials does it hold? Can it release a payment?"
- "What happens when a bank statement is missing at 08:00?"
- "How do you measure whether it was right? Show me the error log from last month."

</Callout>

<Callout type="interview" title="Questions for a treasurer who uses one">

- "What did the tool do this morning before you logged in? What did you change?"
- "Has it ever been wrong in a way that mattered? How did you notice?"
- "What would you never let it do?"
- "If it disappeared tomorrow, what would you have to do by hand?"

</Callout>
