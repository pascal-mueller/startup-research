## Suggested path (2–4 days)

1. **Day 1 — the model.** [What is treasury?](/start) → [Treasury and its neighbours](/start/neighbours) → [How treasury grows](/start/growth) → [Treasury Map](/map) → [Organization & Roles](/org).
2. **Day 2 — the core loop.** [Daily cash positioning](wf:daily-cash-positioning) [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting) → [Liquidity planning](wf:liquidity-planning) → [Payment processing](wf:payment-processing) → [Bank reconciliation](wf:bank-reconciliation). Then read the [Day in the Life](/day) pages.
3. **Day 3 — risk and money markets.** FX, debt, investments and the event workflows (currency shock, liquidity crisis, acquisition).
4. **Day 4 — prepare to interview.** [Interview Prep](/interview), [Who should I interview?](/org/interview-finder), then the separate [Agentic Treasury](/lens) and [Competitor Landscape](/competitors) sections.

## Evidence labels

<Callout type="concept">Established treasury practice or definition, consistent across professional bodies and training material.</Callout>
<Callout type="survey">A finding from a named industry survey. Always cited; note who was surveyed.</Callout>
<Callout type="field">A practitioner pattern — widely reported but not statistically established. Treat as a hypothesis to test in interviews.</Callout>
<Callout type="synthesis">This manual's own reasoning or illustrative construction (e.g. the fictional companies).</Callout>

## Conventions

- Dotted-underlined words are [glossary terms](term:cash-position) — hover for a definition.
- Green arrows are workflow links, e.g. [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting).
- Superscript numbers are citations; hover for the source, click to jump to the list at the end of the page.
- Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd> to search. Mark pages as read at the bottom; progress is stored only in this browser.
