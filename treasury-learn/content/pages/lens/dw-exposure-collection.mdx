## Why this slice

Hedging decisions are only as good as the [exposure](term:fx-exposure) numbers behind them, and the numbers are the
weak part: forecast exposures come from subsidiary templates, balance-sheet exposures from several ERPs with different
mappings, and intercompany balances often disagree between the two sides. Collecting and cleaning this is days of
analyst work a month, it is checkable against ledgers, and a wrong number is caught before it becomes a trade — as long
as a human still decides the hedge.

## A worked month-end

<Example title="October close: three anomalies before Daniel's hedge meeting" company="helvetic" open>

By working day 4 the system has reconciled balance-sheet exposures for seven of eight subsidiaries to their ledgers
(the Chinese entity's ERP export is late) and flags:

| # | Anomaly | Evidence | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HM Czech: EUR 2.4m receivables tagged CZK in the exposure template | ERP open items are EUR-denominated invoices | Local finance confirm template error; corrected |
| 2 | HM Italia: 6-month EUR sales forecast down 30% vs last month | No lost order in the CRM note or Giulia's comments | Giulia: one large order moved to next year — real, not an error |
| 3 | Intercompany: HM Deutschland shows EUR 1.1m payable to HM AG; HM AG shows EUR 0.8m receivable | IC ledgers | Unresolved; excluded from netting until accounting confirms |

**Draft hedge-gap view (for Daniel, not for execution):** after the corrections, 3–6-month EUR net exposure is hedged at
a ratio below the policy corridor; the system lists the forward trades that would bring it back inside, with tenor and
amount, and marks the Italian order change as the reason the gap moved. Daniel reduces the proposed amount because he
doubts the order will return at all, and schedules the trade. Authorised dealers execute on the bank platform; back
office matches the confirmation.

**Recorded:** anomaly #1 true positive; #2 a true change (useful flag, not an error); #3 open; Daniel's reduction and
reason stored against the Italian entity.

</Example>

## Limits

- **Forecast exposures are opinions.** The system can compare them with later invoicing and estimate each entity's
  bias; it cannot make the forecast true.
- **Hedge accounting.** Where hedge accounting is applied, exposure records are part of the documentation auditors test.
  Anything the system changes must be logged and approved.
- **Multi-ERP access** is the practical blocker at mid-market groups; without read access to local ledgers, the system is
  back to reading templates.
