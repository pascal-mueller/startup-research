## Why this slice

Hedging decisions are only as good as the [exposure](term:fx-exposure) numbers behind them, and the numbers are the
weak part: forecast exposures come from subsidiary templates, balance-sheet exposures from several ERPs with different
mappings, and intercompany balances often disagree between the two sides. At Helvetic, Lea spends roughly **WD2–WD6 of
each month** on this — several days of analyst work a month, not a morning — and it is checkable against ledgers. A
wrong number is caught before it becomes a trade [as long as the cycle order holds](why:wd5-wd7-hedge-cycle): submissions by WD5, Lea consolidates, Daniel reviews the report on WD7, and
hedge adjustments run after that review. A "clean report by working day 4" cannot include forecast exposures whose
submission deadline is WD5 — the deadline exists because the stragglers' flows must be in before anyone computes a
hedge.

## A worked month-end

<Example title="The November cycle: three anomalies and a passive over-hedge before Daniel's WD7 review" company="helvetic" open>

**WD2** — Lea sends the exposure template to the eight subsidiaries with the closing rates; deadline WD5 noon
([collection window WD2–WD6](why:wd5-wd7-hedge-cycle)). **By WD3–WD4** the system has reconciled the
balance-sheet exposures for seven of eight subsidiaries to their ledgers (the Chinese entity's ERP export is late) and
flags three anomalies. **WD5–WD6** the submissions arrive and the forecast exposures are cleaned:

| # | Anomaly | Evidence | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HM Czech: EUR 2.4m receivables tagged CZK in the exposure template | ERP open items are EUR-denominated invoices | Local finance confirm template error; corrected |
| 2 | HM Italia: 6-month EUR sales forecast down 30% vs last month | No lost order in the CRM note or Giulia's comments | Giulia: one large order moved to next year — real, not an error |
| 3 | Intercompany: HM Deutschland shows EUR 1.1m payable to HM AG; HM AG shows EUR 0.8m receivable | IC ledgers | Unresolved; excluded from netting until accounting confirms |

**WD7 — the draft hedge-gap view (for Daniel, not for execution):** after the corrections, the system shows coverage
against the [graded bands](why:hedge-bands-graded) per rolling calendar quarter — Q4 2026 at 74% inside its 60–90%
band, and **Q1 2027 at 75% (EUR 30m of forwards against EUR 40m exposure), above its 40–70% band**. It does not
propose buying anything: a ratio above the band is a [passive over-hedge](why:passive-vs-active-breach), and the
policy answer is a correction within the month, reported as passive. The system lists the rebalance — roll about
EUR 3m of Q1 forwards out to Q2, leaving Q1 at 27/40 = 68% and Q2 at 15/29 ≈ 51%, both in band — with the Italian
order change shown as one of the reasons the exposure moved. Daniel decides the roll, halves the Italian forecast
further ("that order does not come back this year — record the override against Giulia"), and schedules it. "No new
hedges this week" stays true: no new *buying*; the rebalance is a roll.

**Execution and records:** Daniel deals within his mandate on the bank platform (up to 12 months and CHF 20m per deal;
options and anything longer need Thomas) and **Lea matches the confirmations and keeps the hedge register** — at a
two-person treasury there is no back office; that function exists at multinationals like [GlobalChem](co:globalchem),
not here. Anomaly #1 recorded as a true positive; #2 as a true change (useful flag, not an error) plus Daniel's
override and its reason; #3 open; forecast-versus-invoiced accuracy starts accumulating per entity.

</Example>

## Limits

- **Forecast exposures are opinions.** The system can compare them with later invoicing and estimate each entity's
  bias; it cannot make the forecast true — and the graded bands exist precisely because the further out the forecast,
  the less of it deserves hedging.
- **Hedge accounting.** Where hedge accounting is applied, exposure records are part of the documentation auditors test
  (at Helvetic the Swiss GAAP FER disclosure rests on "highly probable" future sales). Anything the system changes must
  be logged and approved.
- **Multi-ERP access** is the practical blocker at mid-market groups; without read access to local ledgers, the system is
  back to reading templates.
