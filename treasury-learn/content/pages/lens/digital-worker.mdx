## Why model the assignment, not the feature

A feature description ("AI-powered cash positioning") hides every question that decides whether a treasurer would rely
on it. A digital-worker model forces them into the open. It is a template for **thinking and interviewing**, not a
product spec, and every model below is an illustrative synthesis set at [Helvetic](co:helvetic), where Lea
(analyst) prepares and Daniel (treasury manager) decides — [an ownership that is a control design, not a staffing
detail](why:lens-dw-owner-split).

| Part | The question to answer | Typical trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Human assignment** | What exactly does the manager ask for, by when, and who does it today? | Describing a workflow instead of an assignment |
| **Inputs** | Which system or person provides each input, when does it arrive, how good is it? | Assuming the ERP holds what actually arrives by email |
| **Human reasoning** | What does the treasurer consider — including what they *don't* write down? | Reducing judgment to "analyse the data" |
| **Tools / systems** | Which systems are touched, and is access read, draft or none? | Giving write access to banks "for convenience" |
| **Output** | What artefact does the manager receive? | An answer without evidence or uncertainty |
| **Verification** | How does the expert tell it is right — now, and later? | "The manager reviews it" with no criteria |
| **Execution** | Who acts on it, under which control? | The system as signatory |
| **Feedback loop** | How do corrections and outcomes become training and evaluation data? | Corrections made in the bank portal, invisible to the system |
| **Failure modes & guardrails** | What goes wrong, and what prevents or catches it? | Treating hallucination as the only risk; ignoring stale data, automation bias, injection — and the mundane ones: the system is down at 08:00, or the bank changed its file |

## Four modelled assignments

<DigitalWorkerIndex />

The four were chosen because they combine a recurring assignment, checkable output, a feedback loop, and a place on the
[start-narrow → expand path](/lens/candidates) of a mid-market analyst's week. No payment-release, approval or crisis
assignment was modelled: the lens rates those [hard or dangerous](/lens/candidates).

## Design rules common to all four

<Callout type="synthesis" title="Rules the models apply">

1. **Numbers by code, words by model.** Balances, conversions, variances and policy checks are computed deterministically; the language model selects, explains and drafts.
2. **Evidence or "unexplained".** No explanation without a link to the statement line, invoice or email it rests on. "I don't know" is a valid, and often the most valuable, output.
3. **Maker, never checker.** The system prepares; a named human approves; release follows the existing [bank mandate](term:payment-mandate) and [four-eyes](term:four-eyes-principle) rules. No signing rights, no bank write access.
4. **Autonomy is earned per level.** Each model has an autonomy ladder with an explicit gate (e.g. "ties out every day for two weeks"). Gates are measured on the [evaluation set](term:evaluation-set), not asserted — and [that labelled history belongs to the customer](why:lens-eval-set-ownership), exportable and re-runnable, or the gate cannot be audited and the learning loop becomes lock-in.
5. **Watch the reviewer, too.** [Automation bias](term:automation-bias) is the most likely failure once the system is usually right: track edit rates and seed test items. Where the expert cannot verify every line, verification degrades to sampling and spot-auditing the evidence links ([why sampling is the defensible form](why:compliance-sample-testing)) — say so in the control design, do not pretend to full review.
6. **Inputs are data, not instructions.** Emails and remittance text are an attack surface ([prompt injection](term:prompt-injection), [BEC](term:business-email-compromise)).
7. **Access is granted by gatekeepers, not by the team.** Read access to every bank statement, the treasury mailbox and ERP exports is a bank-contract, data-protection and audit decision ([who signs it off](why:lens-vendor-access-gate)); "read-only first" is not just safe design, it is the only access level that clears procurement quickly.
8. **Degrade loudly, never silently.** Being down at 08:00 and the bank changing its file are scheduled events, not edge cases — the 2009 camt.05x statement versions are discontinued in November 2026[](cite:six-sps-cash-mgmt), which breaks every parser at once. The models must fail to "yesterday's numbers, flagged stale, no proposals", never to a plausible-looking stale table.

</Callout>
