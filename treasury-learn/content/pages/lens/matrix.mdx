<Callout type="synthesis" title="How to read it">

Scores are 1–5 and the author's synthesis ([method](/lens/method)). **Colour shows direction, not size**: green means
the score helps an AI system do the work, red means it gets in the way, grey means context only (current automation and
human involvement). Headers with a dotted red underline are "higher is worse" criteria. Links of the form
`/lens/matrix#workflow-id` open a row directly.

</Callout>

## Patterns worth noticing

- **Sort by "Loop" (feedback).** The top of the list — [cash forecasting](/lens/matrix#cash-forecasting),
  [bank reconciliation](/lens/matrix#bank-reconciliation), [daily cash positioning](/lens/matrix#daily-cash-positioning)
  — are the workflows where outcomes arrive within days and experts correct output routinely. A system can be *measured*
  there. At the bottom are the crises, where there is nothing to learn from.
- **Sort by "Auto" (current automation).** [Payment processing](/lens/matrix#payment-processing) and
  [bank reconciliation](/lens/matrix#bank-reconciliation) are already highly automated by rules. What is left is thin.
- **Sort by "Conseq" (consequences).** The six workflows scored 5 — payment processing, large payment approval, covenant
  monitoring, fraud investigation and the two crises — all involve irrecoverable money or contractual default. None is
  rated a good candidate; covenant monitoring is "possible" only because the calculation can be prepared and checked
  before a human signs the certificate.
- **"Suit" vs "Moat" pull apart.** The highest-suitability workflows have the *lowest* defensibility, because positioning,
  fee checks and reporting are features any TMS or bank can ship. Defensibility shows up where customer-specific
  context accumulates: forecasting overrides, exposure mappings, explanations of recurring exceptions.

<Callout type="warning" title="Disagreeing with the workflow pages">

Each assessment shows the workflow author's own neutral `ai_note` next to the lens view. Where the lens is more
skeptical or more optimistic, the difference is stated. The workflow pages are the reference for *how the work is done*;
this matrix is one reader's view of *what that implies for agents*.

</Callout>
