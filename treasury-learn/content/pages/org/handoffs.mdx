## Why hand-offs matter

A responsibility matrix says who is involved; a hand-off chain says **in what order, with which artefact, through which channel**. Most treasury problems happen at hand-offs, not inside anyone's own task: a template arrives late, an email with a bank-detail change is trusted, a deal is not passed to accounting, a decision waits for someone on holiday.

Every hand-off has four parts worth asking about:

| Part | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** | What starts it? | Thursday 12:00 deadline; a payment run; a subsidiary's email |
| **Artefact** | What is handed over? | Excel template, payment file, deal ticket, bank confirmation, signed resolution |
| **Channel** | How? | Shared folder, email, ERP workflow, TMS, phone |
| **Check** | Who verifies it on receipt, and against what? | Lea compares to last cycle; the payment factory screens against sanctions lists |

<Callout type="field" title="Where the work hides">

Practitioners describe a large part of their week as chasing, checking and re-keying at hand-offs rather than analysing. When you hear "we produce a weekly forecast", the useful follow-up is: "How many people send you something for it, and how many of them do you have to chase?"

</Callout>

The chains below are illustrative, modelled on the reference companies. Step numbers show order; "At other sizes" shows what changes.

## Cash forecasting (13-week)

Modelled on [Helvetic](co:helvetic), fortnightly cycle. Workflow: [Cash forecasting](wf:cash-forecasting).

| # | From | Gives | To | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lea (Treasury Analyst) | Template, deadline, last cycle's variances by entity | Subsidiary finance managers (Giulia, Jens, Wei, …) | Monday, week 1 |
| 2 | Local AP | Scheduled payment runs and large payables | Subsidiary finance manager | Tue–Wed |
| 3 | Local AR / credit control | Open items with realistic receipt dates, disputes | Subsidiary finance manager | Tue–Wed |
| 4 | Subsidiary finance managers | Completed 13-week templates by entity | Lea | Thursday 12:00 CET |
| 5 | Group tax; HR/payroll | Tax payment calendar; payroll and bonus dates | Lea | Standing calendar, updated monthly |
| 6 | Marco (FP&A) | Rolling forecast for weeks 9–13 and months 4–12 | Lea | Monthly |
| 7 | Lea | Consolidated forecast, variance vs last cycle and actuals, questions | Daniel (Treasury Manager) | Friday |
| 8 | Daniel | Challenges on specific lines (e.g. Italy receipts) | Subsidiary finance manager | Friday |
| 9 | Daniel | Adjusted forecast, low point, RCF headroom, proposed actions | Thomas (CFO) | Monthly meeting, or immediately if headroom falls below the policy minimum |
| 10 | Thomas | Decisions on material actions (draw RCF, delay capex, dividend timing) | Daniel; board if needed | As needed |

**At other sizes.** At [Kleio](co:kleio), steps 1–8 collapse into Sarah (CFO) asking Nina for AP/AR lists and department heads for hiring changes, then updating the runway sheet; the board sees it monthly. At [Alpine](co:alpine), the critical hand-off is project managers → Petra (milestone dates), and Martin (CFO) reviews in a Monday meeting. At [GlobalChem](co:globalchem), 40 reporting units submit in a forecasting tool, Priya's team challenges outliers, Anna reviews, and FP&A reconciles the monthly view; the CFO sees a summary.

## Payments (commercial supplier payments)

Workflow: [Payment processing](wf:payment-processing); large items: [Large payment approval](wf:large-payment-approval).

| # | From | Gives | To | Size note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement / business | Purchase order, goods receipt | AP | All sizes (informal at Kleio) |
| 2 | Budget holder | Invoice approval (business approval) | AP | Department head / cost-centre owner |
| 3 | AP | Payment proposal (ERP payment run) | First approver | Nina at Kleio; AP clerk at Alpine; local AP at Helvetic; SSC AP at GlobalChem |
| 4 | First approver | Approved run | Second approver / bank channel | Petra at Alpine; local finance manager at Helvetic |
| 5 | AP or treasury | Total and timing of the run | Whoever funds the account | Treasury funds local accounts at Helvetic; the in-house bank at GlobalChem |
| 6 | Second approver | Release (collective signature) | Bank / payment factory | Sarah at Kleio; Martin at Alpine; local MD at Helvetic (plus Daniel or Thomas above CHF 2m); ERP workflow then payment-factory screening at GlobalChem |
| 7 | Payment factory (large groups) | Screened, routed file; exceptions held | Banks | Marta's team at GlobalChem; not present at smaller companies |
| 8 | Bank | Status reports ([pain.002](term:pain-002)), rejects | AP / payment operations | Rejects handled by whoever sent the file |
| 9 | Bank | Statements | Accounting (reconciliation) and treasury (position) | Next morning |

## FX hedging

Workflow: [FX hedging](wf:fx-hedging) and [FX exposure management](wf:fx-exposure-management).

| # | From | Gives | To | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subsidiaries / FP&A | Forecast foreign-currency flows by month; budget rates | Lea | Monthly |
| 2 | Group accounting | Balance-sheet exposures (foreign-currency receivables, payables, IC loans) | Lea | Month-end |
| 3 | Lea | Net exposure, hedge ratio vs policy corridor, proposed trades | Daniel | Monthly, before the hedging date |
| 4 | Daniel | Decision within policy; request if outside policy | Thomas (only if outside policy) | Same day |
| 5 | Daniel | Trade request; best of several quotes | Banks (RCF lenders) | Execution day |
| 6 | Bank | Confirmation | Lea (not Daniel — segregation of duties) | Same day |
| 7 | Lea | Deal details, confirmation, hedge designation | Claudia's team (group accounting) | Same day / month-end |
| 8 | Daniel | Hedge report (cover ratios, rates achieved vs budget) | Thomas; FP&A | Monthly |

**At other sizes.** At [Kleio](co:kleio) there is usually no hedging — Sarah converts at spot. At [Alpine](co:alpine), Martin trades by phone, Petra checks the confirmation and books it. At [GlobalChem](co:globalchem) exposures are netted by Jonas's desk after the in-house bank and netting, dealers trade on a platform, Marta's back office confirms and settles, and group accounting applies IFRS 9 hedge accounting.

## Funding a subsidiary

Workflow: [Funding a subsidiary](wf:funding-subsidiary).

| # | From | Gives | To | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subsidiary finance manager | Funding need: amount, timing, reason | Daniel | Ideally from the forecast; often an urgent email |
| 2 | Daniel | Proposed instrument (IC loan vs equity), amount, currency, tenor | Group tax | Tax checks rate, thin-cap, withholding tax |
| 3 | Group tax | Rate and constraints | Daniel | Can take days |
| 4 | Daniel | Request for approval (above threshold) | Thomas (CFO) | |
| 5 | Legal | Loan agreement; resolutions for equity | Both entities' signatories | |
| 6 | Lea | Prepared transfer | Daniel + Thomas (release) | Treasury payment, not AP |
| 7 | Lea | Loan record, interest schedule | Claudia's team + subsidiary accounting | Both sides booked the same way |

**At other sizes.** Kleio: Sarah transfers monthly under a cost-plus agreement. Alpine: Martin decides, Petra transfers and books, the tax adviser set the rate once. GlobalChem: most entities simply draw on their in-house bank account; the hand-offs remain for restricted-currency countries and equity injections, where tax, legal and local banks are involved.

## Month-end treasury reporting

Workflow: [Month-end treasury reporting](wf:month-end-reporting).

| # | From | Gives | To | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Banks | Month-end statements and deal confirmations | Lea | Day 1 |
| 2 | Lea | Deal list (FX, deposits, loans), IC loan schedule with interest | Group accounting | Day 1 |
| 3 | Group accounting | Queries, IC mismatches | Lea / subsidiaries | Days 2–3 |
| 4 | Group accounting | Month-end balances, net debt, EBITDA (and covenant definitions semi-annually) | Lea | Days 4–6 |
| 5 | Lea | Draft treasury report: cash, debt, headroom, hedges, counterparties, covenants | Daniel | Day 6 |
| 6 | Daniel | Reviewed report with commentary | Thomas | Day 7 |
| 7 | Thomas / Daniel | Quarterly summary | Audit committee / board | Quarterly |

**At other sizes.** Kleio: a cash/runway page in the board pack from Sarah. Alpine: one page in the controller's management report. GlobalChem: each desk supplies its section from the TMS; Anna signs off; the Audit & Finance Committee receives a quarterly risk report.

## Fraud response (suspected fraudulent payment)

Workflow: [Fraud investigation](wf:fraud-investigation).

| # | From | Gives | To | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whoever notices (AP clerk, local finance, payment operations, the supplier) | Alert: suspicious payment or bank-detail change | Treasury / head of finance | Minutes matter |
| 2 | Treasury (Daniel) or local finance | Recall request with payment details | The sending bank | Immediately — the bank then contacts the receiving bank |
| 3 | Treasury | Hold on related payments and vendor record | AP / payment channel | Same hour |
| 4 | Treasury | Incident summary | CFO | Same day |
| 5 | IT | Mailbox / system compromise analysis | Treasury, legal | Days |
| 6 | Legal / CFO | Police report, insurer notification, disclosure decision | Authorities, insurer, auditor | Days |
| 7 | Accounting | Booking of loss or recovery | — | Month-end |
| 8 | Treasury + AP | Control changes (call-back rule, limits) | CFO; internal audit | Weeks |

**At other sizes.** Kleio and Alpine: the CFO handles steps 2–6 personally with the bank's relationship manager. GlobalChem: the payment factory is often the first to notice; internal audit investigates; Anna decides on bank and external escalation.

## Bank account opening

Workflow: [Bank account management](wf:bank-account-management).

| # | From | Gives | To | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subsidiary / business | Request with reason (new country, new collection channel) | Daniel | Treasury first asks whether an existing account can do the job |
| 2 | Daniel | Proposal: bank, currency, signatories, connectivity | Thomas (approval) | Group policy: no new account without treasury + CFO approval |
| 3 | Legal / company secretary | Commercial-register extract, board resolution, IDs of signatories, UBO information | Lea | KYC is the slow part — weeks |
| 4 | Lea | Completed account-opening and mandate forms | Bank | |
| 5 | Local signatories | Signatures | Bank | Often local directors |
| 6 | Bank | Account details, e-banking or EBICS/SWIFT set-up | Lea; IT | Connectivity test |
| 7 | Lea | New account in the bank account register and multibank tool | Group accounting (GL mapping) | Otherwise statements are not booked |

**At other sizes.** Kleio and Alpine: the CFO and a registered signatory sign; the accountant maps the account. GlobalChem: Marta's team runs KYC and mandates (often via an electronic bank account management process), local directors sign, and internal audit periodically checks the register against bank confirmations.
