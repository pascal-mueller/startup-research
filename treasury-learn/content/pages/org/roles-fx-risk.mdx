## What the role owns

- **Exposure identification:** collecting forecast foreign-currency receipts and payments ([transaction exposure](term:transaction-exposure)) and, where the group hedges it, the net investment in foreign subsidiaries ([translation exposure](term:translation-exposure)).
- **Hedge requirement:** comparing exposure with existing hedges against the policy's [hedge-ratio](term:hedge-ratio) bands by month and currency.
- **Execution:** dealing [forwards](term:forward), [swaps](term:swap) and occasionally [options](term:option) with banks, within the dealing mandate.
- **Hedge effectiveness and documentation:** supplying designations, valuations and effectiveness data so accounting can apply [hedge accounting](term:hedge-accounting).
- **Policy compliance and reporting:** cover ratios, mark-to-market, sensitivities, breaches.
- In many groups, also **interest-rate risk** on debt (fixed/floating mix, [interest-rate swaps](term:interest-rate-swap)).

Job adverts show the accounting interface is a core part of the job, not an afterthought. Disney's FX Risk Management manager advert covers transactional and balance-sheet exposures and partnering with Accounting on hedge designation, valuation reviews, journal entries and audit support[](cite:job-disney-fx-risk).

## Interactions: who gives what, who gets what

| Counterpart | Gives the FX manager | Gets from the FX manager | Where it breaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subsidiaries / local finance** | Monthly forecast of currency receipts and payments by month | Internal hedge rates; sometimes internal forwards via the in-house bank | Forecasts copied from last month; orders in a currency nobody reported; local teams hedging locally against policy |
| **Business units / sales** | Big contracts and tenders in foreign currency, pricing currency | Budget rates, quotes for tenders, advice on currency clauses | A USD 20m contract signed without telling treasury; tender risk (you may or may not win) |
| **Accounting** | Hedge accounting requirements, IFRS 9 / local GAAP rules, audit questions | Deal data, designations, valuations, effectiveness | Documentation not in place at inception = no hedge accounting = P&L volatility |
| **CFO / treasury committee** | Risk appetite, approval of strategy changes | Exposure and cover reports, proposals (e.g. raise months 7–12 cover) | Pressure to "take a view" on the market, which is against most policies |
| **Banks** | Prices, credit lines for derivatives, ISDA/master agreements | Deal flow (part of the [wallet](term:wallet-share)) | Credit-line limits per bank; wallet politics override best price |
| **Treasury operations** | Confirmations, settlement, static data | Deal tickets | Unmatched confirmations; wrong settlement instructions |
| **Cash / liquidity desk** | Currency needs from funding (FX swaps) | Swaps for liquidity management | Double hedging when two desks trade the same exposure |

## Where judgment sits

- **Believing the exposure.** Whether a subsidiary's USD forecast is real, and how far out to trust it. Most groups hedge less the further out the month.
- **Timing within the band.** Mechanical layering or discretionary timing. Many policies prescribe layering precisely to remove market-timing judgment.
- **Instrument choice.** Forwards are the default. Options cost a premium and need approval and understanding at CFO level.
- **Bank allocation.** Best price, or the bank that needs wallet share because it lends in the RCF.
- **What to tell the CFO after a big move.** See the [currency shock](wf:currency-shock) workflow.

## Interview implications

<Callout type="interview">

Best for: exposure data quality, hedge programme mechanics, dealing, bank pricing, the accounting interface. In smaller companies, ask the same questions to the treasury manager or CFO.

- "Where does your exposure number come from, and how wrong was it last year?"
- "What happens when a subsidiary signs a big foreign-currency contract without telling you?"
- "Which part of hedge accounting creates the most work?"
- "How do you choose which bank gets a trade?"

Not for: daily cash, payments, bank connectivity.

</Callout>
