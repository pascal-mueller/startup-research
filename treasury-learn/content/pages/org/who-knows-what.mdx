## How to choose interviewees

<Callout type="synthesis" title="Five rules of thumb">

1. **Go to the person who does the work, then one level up.** For a workflow, interview whoever performs it (often an analyst or manager), then whoever decides on its output (manager or head of treasury). Each explains what the other can't.
2. **Adjust for company size.** At an SME the CFO and head of accounting do treasury work, so interview them. At a multinational the CFO is far from the work; interview desk heads and analysts.
3. **Seniority buys the why, not the how.** Senior people explain decisions, trade-offs and budgets. Junior people explain files, steps, timings and workarounds.
4. **Include one neighbour.** Treasury's problems often start elsewhere: a subsidiary's forecast, AP's vendor data, accounting's close. One interview outside treasury shows the hand-offs.
5. **Check the pattern in the first two minutes.** Ask about reporting line, team size and what they did this morning. See the [Treasury Manager page](/org/roles-treasury-manager).

</Callout>
