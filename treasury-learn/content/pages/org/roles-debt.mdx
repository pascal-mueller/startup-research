## Inside treasury or next to it?

| Model | Where you find it | What the team does | Who does M&A |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Funding inside treasury** | Most European groups ([GlobalChem](co:globalchem): Olivier reports to Anna) | Bonds, [commercial paper](term:commercial-paper), [RCF](term:rcf), [private placements](term:private-placement) / [Schuldschein](term:schuldschein), rating agencies, covenant data | A separate M&A team under the CFO or strategy; treasury finances the deals |
| **Corporate Finance next to treasury** | Some groups, often US or deal-heavy companies | Capital structure, M&A, sometimes investor relations; treasury handles execution and liquidity | Corporate Finance |
| **CFO plus treasury manager** | Mid-market ([Helvetic](co:helvetic)) | Thomas leads negotiations; Daniel prepares, manages drawings and covenants, and will run the 2028 bond refinancing process with the banks | CFO with external advisers |
| **CFO and the house bank** | SME ([Alpine](co:alpine)) | Martin negotiates the CHF 12m credit line and plant mortgage; Petra tracks drawings | CFO with advisers, rarely |

<Callout type="confusion" title="'Corporate finance' has two meanings">

In treasury, "corporate finance" often means **funding the company**: debt, ratings, capital structure. In banking and advisory it usually means **M&A and deal advisory**. The ACT's body of knowledge includes corporate finance alongside funding, liquidity and risk management[](cite:act-what-is-treasury). Ask which one your interviewee means.

</Callout>

## What the work involves

- **Funding plan and maturity profile.** When does debt mature, and how will it be refinanced? At Helvetic the CHF 100m bond due 2028 is the defining item. Treasurers usually start refinancing well over a year ahead. One reason: debt due within 12 months is shown as a current liability, which auditors, banks and rating agencies notice.
- **Facilities.** RCF renewals and extensions, bank syndicate management, pricing, documentation with Legal.
- **Capital markets.** Bond issuance (mandating banks, documentation, roadshows), CP programme management (weekly issuance, dealer panel).
- **Ratings.** Annual reviews with rating agencies, credit-metric projections, rating-sensitive decisions (GlobalChem at BBB+ / Baa1).
- **[Covenants](term:covenant).** Calculations with accounting, compliance certificates, headroom projections. See [covenant monitoring](wf:covenant-monitoring).
- **Interest-rate risk** on the debt portfolio, sometimes shared with the risk desk.

## Interview implications

<Callout type="interview">

Funding work is **episodic**: a bond every few years, an RCF renewal every few years, CP weekly in large groups. It involves few people, high value and a lot of documents and relationships. It is a poor fit for workflow-volume questions and a good fit for "walk me through your last refinancing: who was involved, how long did it take, what was painful?"

At mid-market companies, ask the CFO and treasury manager together. At multinationals, the Head of Corporate Finance or a capital-markets manager.

</Callout>
