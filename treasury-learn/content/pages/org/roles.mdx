## Read titles with suspicion

Three rules before the list:

1. **The same title means different jobs at different sizes.** A Treasury Manager at a CHF 650m group can be the whole treasury ([Daniel](/org/roles-treasury-manager)); at a CHF 7bn group, a specialist on one desk.
2. **Where a role doesn't exist, the work still does.** At an SME with no treasurer, the CFO decides and the head of accounting executes. Each role page has a "does this role exist?" table.
3. **Ask what they did this morning, not what they are called.** Reporting line and team size tell you more than the title.

<Callout type="field" title="Typical ladder">

In mid-sized and large treasuries the progression is usually Treasury Analyst → Senior Analyst / Treasury Manager (or Cash Manager) → desk head (Head of Cash & Liquidity, Head of Financial Risk, Head of Corporate Finance) → Group Treasurer. The ACT's competency framework describes a similar progression through tactical, operational, managerial and strategic levels[](cite:act-competency-framework). Many people enter treasury from audit, accounting, banking or FP&A[](cite:learnsignal-career-2026). Small companies have no ladder: one person, or none.

</Callout>
