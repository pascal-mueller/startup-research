## Two relationship managers, two sides

<Callout type="confusion" title="Bank RM vs corporate bank relationship manager">

- The **bank's relationship manager** (the "RM", "coverage banker", *Firmenkundenberater*) works for the bank. They sell credit and services, bring in product specialists (cash management, FX, debt capital markets) and coordinate KYC. Job titles such as "Treasury Relationship Manager" are usually bank sales roles, focused on selling treasury-management and deposit products and winning clients[](cite:job-bank-treasury-rm).
- The **corporate side** decides which banks the company uses and for what. In most companies this is not a job title but part of the CFO's and treasurer's work.

</Callout>

## What bank relationship management involves

| Activity | What it means | Who does it at Helvetic | Who does it at GlobalChem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bank panel strategy** | Which banks, how many, for what. Credit providers usually expect ancillary business | Thomas and Daniel (5 banks) | Anna and Olivier (about 20 banks, 7 core RCF banks) |
| **[Wallet-share](term:wallet-share) tracking** | What each bank earns from the company (fees, FX, deposits, DCM) vs what it provides (credit) | Daniel, roughly, in Excel, once a year | Olivier's team, quarterly |
| **Allocating [ancillary business](term:ancillary-business)** | FX trades, cash-management mandates, bond mandates | Daniel for FX; Thomas for mandates | Anna / Olivier; the treasury committee for big mandates |
| **Fee review** | Checking bank charges against agreed pricing | Daniel, quarterly | Marta's team and Ben's data; see [bank fee analysis](wf:bank-fee-analysis) |
| **Facilities** | RCF renewals, guarantee lines, derivative credit lines | Daniel and Thomas | Olivier |
| **[KYC](term:kyc), mandates, signatories** | Documents, board resolutions, signatory changes | Daniel, with local finance for subsidiaries | Marta's team |
| **Service issues** | Failed files, delayed payments, onboarding | Daniel / Lea with the bank's service desk | Marta, Ben |
| **Annual bank reviews / scorecards** | Formal assessment of each bank | Informal | Yes, with scorecards |

Practitioner guidance puts bank relationship management under the corporate treasurer. The tools are share-of-wallet analysis, regular communication and bank scorecards[](cite:ctm-brm-leading-practices), and the "underfed / on target / overfed" view of each bank[](cite:tt-share-of-wallet). KYC is a large operational burden: in one vendor-sponsored survey of 250 treasurers, 92% said banks asked for the same information more than once[](cite:encompass-kyc-2025).

## Interview implications

<Callout type="interview">

- To understand how banks are chosen and paid, interview the **CFO** (SME, mid-market) or the **Group Treasurer / Head of Corporate Finance** (multinational).
- To understand KYC and account-admin pain, interview **treasury operations** or, at SMEs, the **head of accounting**.
- Bank RMs are useful for seeing many companies (they know what peers do) but are sales-driven. Treat their view of "what treasurers need" as a vendor view.
- Questions: "How did you decide on your current banks?" "How do you know what each bank earns from you?" "When did a bank last threaten to reduce a facility, and why?" "How long did the last KYC refresh take, and who did it?"

</Callout>
